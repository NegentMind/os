# Trademark Policy

This policy covers the names and marks used by Negent Mind:

- **"Negent Mind"**, the **NM monogram** and the **NEGENT MIND wordmark**.
- The product names **"VantaDB"** and **"Scopegrain"**.

Negent Mind is an independent lab, not yet an incorporated company. These marks are **not
yet registered**; this policy describes how we ask them to be used and the rights we
intend to keep.

## Code license vs. trademarks

Our code is licensed under the Apache License 2.0. That license covers the code, not the
names. Section 6 of the Apache License 2.0 states:

> This License does not grant permission to use the trade names, trademarks, service
> marks, or product names of the Licensor, except as required for reasonable and customary
> use in describing the origin of the Work and reproducing the content of the NOTICE file.

You can use, modify and redistribute the code under the license. Using the names is
governed by this policy.

## What you may do

- Make factual, nominative references: "built with VantaDB", "a plugin for VantaDB",
  "compatible with Scopegrain", "an article about Negent Mind".
- Link to the official repositories and documentation.
- Reproduce the `NOTICE` file and describe the origin of the code, as the license
  requires.

## What you may not do without written permission

- Imply endorsement, sponsorship or affiliation with Negent Mind that does not exist.
- Distribute forks or modified versions under these names. **Rename your fork** and say
  it is based on the original (for example, "based on VantaDB").
- Use the marks, or confusingly similar names, in a product, company, package or domain
  name.
- Present a modified version as the official one.
- Alter the NM monogram or the NEGENT MIND wordmark, or combine them with your own marks.

## Questions and permission requests

until the negentmind.com domain is live.
